# Operator logos

Egged, Dan, Nativ Express and Metropoline were downloaded from the operators' own
websites (no public brand/press kit was found for any of them); Egged Taavura, NTT,
Superbus and Kavim were supplied by the project owner. The marks are trademarks of their owners; they are used here
only to identify the operator of a bus line next to its number. Check each
owner's terms before distributing the watch app (e.g. via the Connect IQ store).

Operators without a logo (GB Tours, Galim, Carmelit, the rest) fall back to the
coloured code badge in `Operators.mc`.

`watch/resources*/logos/*.png` are generated by `tools/logos/build.py` from these SVGs: crop to the visible
area, scale to 11% of the screen height per screen size (218/260/390), keep aspect ratio.
